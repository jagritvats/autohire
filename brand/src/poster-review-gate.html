Free software · runs on your machine
The review gate

It fills the form. You press submit.

The tedious part, done from what you already stored. The decision stays with you.

  1. 1
    It plans

    jobflow apply is a dry run: each field, and the value it would type. Nothing is filled or sent.

  2. 2
    It fills

    With --fill, a browser on your machine opens the form, types your details and attaches your résumé.

  3. 3
    It stops

    At the review screen. You read every field, then press submit yourself, or close the tab.

There is no auto-submit. Not a setting, not a flag, not a hidden mode: the form filler has no code path that presses the button.
