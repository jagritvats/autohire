The mark

A J and a full stop. JobFlow finds, ranks and fills, then it stops. The next move is yours, so the stop carries the accent, the colour that means “you can act here” everywhere in the product.

The J is Fraunces (soft, optical size 72) drawn as outlines; the stop sits on its baseline. One stop per lockup: the wordmark gets it only when it stands alone.

Clear space

¼¼

Keep a quarter of the tile’s height clear on every side, for the tile and for every lockup (measured from the tile). Nothing but the page colour goes there.

Sizes · two optical masters

The text master (heavier J, bigger stop) is for 32 px and below: favicons, the extension, the app header. The display master is for 48 px and up. Smallest: mark 16 px / 5 mm; horizontal lockup 20 px / 6 mm tall.

Lockups

Horizontal (default) · stacked · wordmark alone · on night · one colour, knocked out. Files: brand/logo/jobflow-{horizontal,stacked,wordmark,mark}[-dark|-mono-black|-mono-white].svg

In the product

The app header draws the mark from theme tokens: the tile is the header’s ink, the J is cut out of it, and the stop is the accent. So it fits Editorial, Classic and Spark, light and dark, without its own colours.

The stop always takes the accent of the surface under it: ember on an ink tile (6.3 : 1), vermilion on a paper tile (4.8 : 1). Vermilion on ink is only 3.2 : 1. One accent per piece; status colours stay in the product.
